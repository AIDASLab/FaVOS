Volumetric J&F\(\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}\)

We compare masks as spatio-temporal volumes. Jointly empty frames add no reward; false positives add union without overlap.

J&F_v = (J_v + F_v)/2\[\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}_v=\tfrac12(\mathcal{J}_v+\mathcal{F}_v)\]

J_v = Σ_t |M_t ∩ predicted M_t| / Σ_t |M_t ∪ predicted M_t|\[\mathcal{J}_v=\frac{\sum_t\|\mathcal{M}_t\cap\hat{\mathcal{M}}_t\|_0}{\sum_t\|\mathcal{M}_t\cup\hat{\mathcal{M}}_t\|_0}\]

M_t\(\mathcal{M}_t\) is ground truth; predicted M_t\(\hat{\mathcal{M}}_t\) is the prediction. J_v\(\mathcal{J}_v\) measures volume overlap; F_v\(\mathcal{F}_v\) measures spatio-temporal boundary agreement, with same-frame matching by default (δ_t = 0\(\delta_t=0\)).

Robustness to frame weighting

Across linear, square-root, normalized logarithmic, and uniform frame weights w(u_t)\(w(u_t)\) (u_t\(u_t\): union size), FaVOS-40 rankings stay fixed; FaVOS-20 only swaps DeAOT-L and SAMURAI-L. SAM 3 and SeC remain the top two; empty scores zero throughout (Section 6.4).

Balanced J&F\(\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}\) still gives empty 50.0 on sequences with both presence states. Present-frame J&F\(\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}\) and presence F1\(\mathrm{F}_1\) provide diagnostics without a unique ranking (Appendix D).

Abstract

Targets in long videos often appear intermittently. We introduce FaVOS to evaluate video object segmentation under low temporal visibility. Here, frame-wise J&F\(\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}\) can favor an empty-mask predictor over SAM 3 by rewarding correct absence. Volumetric J&F\(\mathcal{J}\&\mathcal{F}\) evaluates mask sequences as spatio-temporal volumes, preserving sensitivity to segmentation quality and temporal structure without explicit absence rewards.
